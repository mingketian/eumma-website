Emory Undergraduate MatheMatics Association (EUMMA) is a student organization
dedicated to building the undergraduate mathematics community at Emory through
academic panels, study sessions, competition and AI training, and social events.

Join EUMMA Our Team

What is EUMMA?

We host academic panels, study sessions, AI competition training, and game nights. All of our events are open to everyone: math majors and non-math majors, undergraduate and graduate. Register on The Hub, join the GroupMe or WeChat, and come to something.

Explore AI

New this year. We work one Kaggle competition per semester as a group, starting from an empty notebook, plus short workshops on the linear algebra and optimization underneath it. No prior coding is assumed.

    Research Areas in the Department

    The research areas in the Department of Mathematics, and the courses that lead into each. If you want to do research in one of them, this is where to start reading.

    Algebra and Number Theory

    Leads in via MATH 250 → 421 · 422

    Structures of solution sets, algebraic geometry, and integers with an emphasis on primes.

    Rohini Ramadas · Parimala Raman · Robert Silversmith · Suresh Venapally

    Department page ↗

    Analysis and Differential Geometry

    Leads in via MATH 250 → 411 · 412 · 318 · 344

    Change, limits and continuity, extended to real and complex analysis, functional analysis, and the geometry of curves, surfaces and manifolds.

    David Borthwick · Maja Taskovic · Yiran Wang · Shanshuang Yang

    Department page ↗

    CODES — Computational and Data Enabled Science

    Leads in via MATH 221 → 315 · 346 · 351 · 361

    The mathematics of deep learning, data assimilation and uncertainty quantification, applied to medical imaging, cardiac modeling, storm surge and disease outbreaks. The largest group in the department, and the one closest to what we do in the Kaggle sessions.

    Julianne Chung · Matthias Chung · Bree Ettinger · Manuela Girotti · Manuela Manetta · Talea Mayo · James Nagy · Levon Nurbekyan · Lars Ruthotto · Marco Tezzele · Yuanzhe Xi

    Department page ↗

    Discrete Mathematics and Combinatorics

    Leads in via MATH 250

    Graphs, extremal problems, and the interaction between convex sets and lattices. The area closest to competition problems.

    Cosmin Pohoata · Aleksandr Polianskii · Vojtech Rodl · Robert Roth · Liana Yepremyan

    Department page ↗

    Getting in

    Undergraduate Research Opportunities

    Every route into research open to an Emory undergraduate, with the one thing about each that is easy to miss.

    Start here

    Directed Reading Program

    One credit, one graduate mentor, one topic you chose. The lowest-commitment way to find out whether you like research at all.

    math.emory.edu/~drp →
    Summer · paid

    SURE

    Ten weeks of full-time research with a faculty mentor, a stipend and campus housing. It also satisfies the XA general education requirement. Decisions go out in early March, so the mentor conversation happens in the fall.

    SURE at Emory College →
    Summer · in the department

    CMDS REU / RET

    The department’s own summer program in Computational Mathematics for Data Science, run by the CODES group. Closest fit if the Kaggle sessions were the part you liked.

    math.emory.edu/cmds-reuret →

    Questions

    FAQ

    Do I have to be a math major?

    No. Most members study Mathematics, Applied Mathematics or AMS, but plenty do not. Every event is open to any Emory student.

    Is there a fee?

    No. Food at events is paid for by the club.

    How often do you meet?

    About two events a month during term, plus study sessions around midterms and finals.

    Can my organization co-host with EUMMA?

    Yes. Panels, workshops and joint socials. Email the board.
